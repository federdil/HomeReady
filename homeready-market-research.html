The UK residential property market is the UK's largest retail transaction category — yet no single product owns the end-to-end first-time buyer journey. Existing tools address isolated moments (search, mortgage calculators, generic legal info) but leave buyers navigating the most stressful, expensive stages entirely alone. The government's own 2026 Home Buying Reform roadmap explicitly calls out the lack of buyer-side transparency as a systemic failure.

Gap analysis
Biggest pain points the market still hasn't solved

Assessed across all currently available UK tools — portals, calculators, legal info sites, and AI assistants.

S4
Legal documents written for lawyers, not buyers
Buyers receive draft contracts, title registers, and search results during conveyancing and have no way to understand them without paying solicitor hourly rates. AI tools for this exist for solicitors (Moverly, Writford, Legalito) — none are designed for buyers.
Critical gap
S2
Listing language that systematically misleads
Every portal shows listings as written by agents, for sellers. Jitty offers conversational search, but no tool decodes euphemisms, flags missing info, or gives a trust score. Buyers arrive at viewings misled — every time.
Critical gap
S2
Neighbourhood intelligence scattered across dozens of sources
TfL, Ofsted, flood risk, crime data, planning portals — all public, none synthesised per property for a buyer. Portals offer superficial local data; nothing provides a synthesised, buyer-oriented briefing from live APIs.
Critical gap
S4
Survey reports that cause panic without context
RICS Level 2 and Level 3 surveys return dense amber/red findings. No consumer product categorises these by severity, explains implications, or advises whether findings warrant renegotiation. Buyers either over-react or under-react.
Critical gap
S1
True cost of buying never presented in one place
SDLT, solicitor fees, survey, broker, moving costs — calculators exist for each individually. No tool combines them into a single true acquisition cost from a listing URL. Buyers discover the full picture at offer stage when they've emotionally committed.
Partially served
S6
Post-completion admin with real financial penalties if missed
SDLT must be paid within 14 days of completion. Land Registry, utilities, insurance — no single party briefs buyers comprehensively. Generic moving checklists exist; deadline-driven, completion-date-anchored trackers do not.
Partially served
S3
Offer price anchored on emotion, not comparable data
Land Registry data is public but requires interpretation most buyers lack. Rightmove and Zoopla show sold prices but without a plain-English "is this fair?" layer. Buyers routinely overpay by £5–30K or underbid unnecessarily.
Partially served
S2
Leasehold traps invisible until after solicitor instruction
Most leasehold risk (short lease, ground rent escalation, high service charges) is buried in listing small print or only surfaces at conveyancing. Buyers with flats — 90%+ of London stock — have no early-warning system.

Positioning
HomeReady's value proposition
Core proposition
"The knowledgeable friend who's been through it — available at every stage of your first home, for free."

Every other product in this market either serves the seller's interests (portals), stops at search (Jitty), provides only static guidance (MoneyHelper, Which?), or is built for professionals not buyers (AI conveyancing tools). HomeReady is the only product that follows the buyer from "what can I afford?" through to "what do I do in the first 14 days of owning?" — using AI to do what previously required a solicitor friend, a financially savvy parent, and hours of research.

Buyer-first
No seller funding, no estate agent affiliation, no mortgage affiliate model. Pure buyer advocacy — the only product explicitly on the buyer's side.
End-to-end
Stages 1–6: financial readiness through post-completion. The only UK product that doesn't drop buyers after they find a property.
AI that acts
Not a chatbot. Actual document analysis, live API orchestration, and clause-level legal explanation — capabilities previously gated behind professional fees.
Strategic lens
Where HomeReady should double down
🎯
The legal document gap is the biggest defensible moat
AI conveyancing tools are being built for solicitors at speed (Moverly, Legalito, Writford). None are for buyers. HomeReady getting to buyer-facing document analysis first — and doing it well — creates a category before enterprise tools pivot to the consumer space.
Priority
🎯
Journey continuity is the structural advantage
Every competitor solves one moment. HomeReady's journey tracker makes it structurally sticky — users return at each stage. The post-completion checklist alone captures a moment no other product touches at all.
Priority
⚠
Jitty is the closest threat — but still search-only
Jitty launched its conversational AI buyer assistant in March 2026. It's well-funded and explicitly buyer-first. Watch for expansion post-offer into guidance territory. HomeReady needs to own Stages 3–6 before Jitty decides to go there.
Watch
⚠
Government reform is tailwind — and potential threat
The 2026 Home Buying Reform roadmap mandates standardised upfront property data and sales packs. This could make some HomeReady features redundant — or dramatically increase demand for tools that help buyers interpret more upfront data.
